PHASE: 01

“Now here we go dropping science, dropping it all over / Like bumping around the town, like when you're driving a Range Rover / Expanding the horizons and expanding the parameters / Expanding the rhymes of sucka MC amateurs” -Beastie Boys

Solomon Rosenthal did not seem like a high ranking member of a cult. He pays his taxes, goes to synagogue, and gives 110% when it comes to his driver's ed students.

But on Saturday nights, he becomes King Solomon, holding dominion over Jinn. King Solomon stands in the center of the twelve silent G.E.N.I members arranged in a dodecagram shape on the upper floor of Atomic Pizza.

Mediocre pizza. 2.8 Yelp Score. King Solomon rolls the 12-sided die. It hits the floor with a clatter. “Brother Michael, the die has decided. Disengage from your trauma.”

Brother Michael speaks of a fight with his wife. Small things. Shoes. Escalation. Coldness. After 12 minutes and 12 seconds, the watch goes off. Michael’s eyes flash red. Archaic Arabic spills out. Noxious brimstone.

“Your wish, Brother Michael?!” Michael screams a single thought: I wish my wife and I never argued. The glowing red vanishes.

Chapter 2: Genie in a Bottle. A weather worn pamphlet is taped haphazardly to a street light. What does G.E.N.I stand for? Simple! It can be broken up into four fundamental forces!

G: Gravity / E: Electromagnetism / N: Nuclear / I: Interactions. Next to this is a halftone image of a 1950s man with a pipe.

Father Castellano walks by and sneers at the pamphlet. He enters the rectory for The Parish of St. Barbara the Martyr. He begins taking out spaghetti noodles and sauce.

Frantic knocking at the door. Castellano adjusts his wire rim glasses. “Can I help you?” he says as he delicately opens the door.

“Father, I need help. Bad.” says the gruff looking man wearing a zip-up Mets hoodie and a face of fear. He glances over his own shoulder nervously.

“Ah, Salvatore. Of course. I’ll take you to the confessionals.” says Father Castellano in a reassuring tone.
