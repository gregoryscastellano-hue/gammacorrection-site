HITTING THE NEEDLE: CHAPTER I

“Now here we go dropping science, dropping it all over / Like bumping around the town, like when you're driving a Range Rover / Expanding the horizons and expanding the parameters / Expanding the rhymes of sucka MC amateurs” -Beastie Boys

Solomon Rosenthal did not seem like a high ranking member of a cult. He pays his taxes, goes to synagogue, and gives 110% when it comes to his driver's ed students.

But on Saturday nights, he becomes King Solomon, holding dominion over Jinn.

King Solomon stands in the center of the twelve silent G.E.N.I members arranged in a dodecagram shape on the upper floor of Atomic Pizza. Mediocre pizza. 2.8 Yelp Score.

King Solomon rolls the 12-sided die. It hits the floor with a clatter. “Brother Michael, the die has decided. Disengage from your trauma.”

“I don't know where to begin. I got into a nasty fight with my wife this week. It always starts with something small... Then it escalates.”

After 12 minutes and 12 seconds, King Solomon’s wristwatch goes off. Brother Michael’s transformation is slow at first, then all at once. His eyes flash red.

The smell of mediocre pizza is replaced by noxious brimstone. “Your wish, Brother Michael! What is your wish?!”

I wish my wife and I never argued. The glowing red vanishes, leaving a distraught, sweating Michael collapsed in his chair.
