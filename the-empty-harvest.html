SET #0001: THE EMPTY HARVEST
1

[PARTS REQUIRED: FEAR, SOIL, BONE]

The sun dragged slowly upward from the horizon into a listless sky, its light too tired to reach the ground. The futile light faintly illuminated black smoke as it wobbled pathetically from weather-gnawed chimneys.

Slowly and routinely, the village of peasants began exiting their homes from their cracked and broken doors, the clothes on their bodies carrying the filth of years without a proper wash.

The expression on their faces was one of tired apathy, as this same routine had occurred hundreds of times in the past, and hundreds again that hadn’t yet arrived. Slowly they marched towards the barren field.
