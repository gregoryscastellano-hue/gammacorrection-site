It all started in Krios of '27. That was when the COR sent me to Ontembaar. I got word from my superior Enguerrand Badeaux. He walked into my office with the stride of a man who felt he could reshape the world with his word alone. "Dabinett, have you been drinking?" he asked, the disgust in his tone palpable. I had. I felt no reason to lie to him. "Yes." Enguerrand's face didn't change. "I know. You're a slave to your impulses." This was his way of trying to reshape the world, specifically my behavior. He always thought appealing to the tradition of the Tanner thinker Glædwine was the best way to get through to me. It wasn't, and I could clearly see through it. "There's beauty in being a slave. You're fed, you have shelter. What's a little manual labor?" Clearly wanting to change the subject, Enguerrand proceeded. "They want you to go to Ontembaar. The Council is making a proclamation soon about the mandate." "Why do they want me?" I smiled. I just liked to hear him say it. Enguerrand's eyelid twitched. "They want you on the ground to plan the Cardónian city." "What about Henri? Surely the Cardónians want a city with some le montagnard influence?" Enguerrand was livid. "They want you." "Why?" Enguerrand was already at the door. "You're to be at the docks at 0500. Don't oversleep." I took a look at the map on my wall. Ontembaar is three weeks by ship from Linolia. I don't like ships. They're so unplanned. A hundred and ten people on average, all trapped together in a metal rookery. Nevertheless, they wanted me, like Enguerrand said. I could have never resisted the siren call of having tens of thousands all using my plans. A beautifully calibrated machine that could not exist without me. I began packing my tools.
